# Presbyterian VTD labels

The 2020 Census VTD labels for Williamson `009726` and Weakley `009510` name polling sites at First Presbyterian Church in Franklin and Trinity Presbyterian Church in Martin. The [PCUSA directory entry for First Presbyterian](https://pcusa.org/congregation/first-church-franklin-tn) matches the Franklin polling-site address, 101 Legends Club Lane. Its [Trinity entry](https://pcusa.org/congregation/trinity-church-martin-tn) identifies the Martin congregation. The friendly-name crosswalk appends `, PCUSA` to those two VTD labels, and `Scripts/build_tn_precinct_friendly_names.js` preserves the suffix on rebuild.

The Kelso and Gibson labels already say Cumberland Presbyterian Church, identifying their denomination in the church name; no extra suffix is added.
